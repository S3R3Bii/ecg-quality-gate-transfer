Contains information from the [MIT-BIH Noise Stress Test Database1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/nstdb/1.0.0/) and [MIT-BIH Arrhythmia Database1.0.0](https://physionet.org/content/mitdb/1.0.0/), made available under the [Open Data Commons Attribution License1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). The two dataset license pages are linked in the protocol. Derived per-window features and produced figures retain this notice and source/version lineage. Original authors: George Moody and Roger Mark. Cite Moody, Muldrow and Mark(1984), Moody and Mark(2001), and the current PhysioNet platform citation linked from the source pages. No endorsement is implied.

The authored code's MIT license applies to authored code only. Source rights, including ODC attribution and any rights beyond database copyright, remain with their owners. No raw waveforms, raw header comments/demographics/medications, annotations, third-party articles, publisher pages, protected clinical datasets, credentials or private records enter the public package. Only fixed public record identifiers, sanitized native encoding metadata, derived numerical features and new analyses may be published after checks. Formal-publication projects including Azure optimization remain private.
